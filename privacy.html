Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Covekin ("we", "us", "our") is responsible for the personal data described in this policy and is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have in relation to your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Email address and password (used for authentication), or, if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, the account identifier and email address that Apple or Google shares with us (with Sign in with Apple this may be a private relay address)
  • Username, display name, and profile photo
  • Date of birth (used to confirm you meet the minimum age and to place your account in the correct age group: junior for under 18, or adult for 18 and over)
  • Parent or guardian name and email address, for users in countries where parental consent is required (see section 4)
  • Subscription tier and payment status (payment details are handled by Apple or Google — we do not store card numbers)

Content You Create

  • Character profiles, descriptions, and portrayal notes
  • Photos and videos you post, including in kinship requests
  • Messages sent to other users
  • Audio from live voice calls, which is transmitted in real time and is not recorded
  • Game results, scores, and activity within Cove Games
  • Profile bio, interests, and preferences

Usage Data

  • Features used and screens visited within the app
  • Device type and operating system (for compatibility)
  • Crash reports and error logs (to improve stability)
  • Your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow it (see section 8)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Covekin service
  • Verify your age and manage access to age-appropriate content
  • Match you with compatible Kins based on your preferences and characters
  • Process subscription payments and manage your account
  • Send important service notifications (account security, policy updates)
  • Send you our newsletter, only if you opt in — you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter email
  • Show advertisements in the app (see section 8)
  • Automatically screen uploaded images for content that breaks our rules
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and violations of our Terms and Conditions
  • Improve our app through analysis of aggregated, anonymised usage patterns

We will not use your personal information for purposes incompatible with those described above without first obtaining your consent.

Legal bases (users in Europe)

  • Contract — to create your account and provide the service you signed up for, including posts, messages, matching, games, voice calls, and subscriptions
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the community safe (age checks, moderation, and abuse prevention), to fix crashes, to understand how the app is used so we can improve it, and to show non-personalised ads
  • Consent — to send you our newsletter and, for adult users, to show personalised ads; you can withdraw consent at any time
  • Legal obligation — where the law requires us to keep or disclose information

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not share your information with data brokers. Advertising partners receive only the limited information described in section 8.

We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who assist us in operating the app — these providers are contractually bound to protect your data:
    • Supabase — database, account authentication, and file storage
    • Apple (Sign in with Apple) and Google (Google Sign-In) — only if you choose to sign in with them
    • Yoti — facial age estimation (see section 4)
    • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Rekognition) — automated screening of uploaded images for content that breaks our rules
    • Daily.co — audio for live voice calls (calls are not recorded)
    • RevenueCat — subscription management (payments themselves are processed by Apple or Google)
    • Resend — delivery of account emails, such as email verification and password reset
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of push notifications
    • Sentry — crash reports and error logs
    • PostHog (EU hosting) — in-app usage analytics
    • Advertising partners — to show ads in the app (see section 8)
  • With law enforcement or regulatory bodies when required by applicable law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where your data would be transferred to the successor entity under the same privacy protections

4. Age Verification and Minors

Covekin requires users to meet the minimum age for the country they live in, which ranges from 13 to 18. The full list is in section 2 of our Terms and Conditions. We use date of birth collected at sign-up to enforce this requirement.

Users under 18 have junior accounts. Junior and adult accounts are kept completely separate: adults cannot find, message, or call junior users, and junior users cannot find, message, or call adults. Junior accounts start with more restrictive settings.

Users who sign up with a junior account complete a one-time facial age estimation, provided by Yoti, before they can start private chats. We may ask other users to complete the same check if there is reason to believe the age they gave is incorrect. You take a selfie in the app and Yoti estimates your age from it. No images are stored: Covekin never keeps the selfie, and Yoti deletes it as soon as the estimate is made. We keep only whether the check was completed and the date it took place.

In Brazil, Colombia and South Africa, a parent or legal guardian must give consent before a junior account can be used (for users under 16 in Brazil, and under 18 in Colombia and South Africa). We collect the parent or guardian's name and email address to request and record that consent. In Brazil, the account of a user under 16 is linked to the parent or guardian who gave consent. A parent or guardian can withdraw consent or ask us to delete their child's account at any time by contacting support@covekin.app.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under the minimum age, we will delete that information promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has created an account should contact us at support@covekin.app.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase, with data centres located in the European Union (Ireland). We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Some of our service providers are based outside Europe, including in the United States. When your data is transferred to them, we rely on safeguards recognised under European law, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login credentials confidential.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and associated data
  • Export — request a portable copy of your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain uses of your data, such as for analytics

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@covekin.app. We will respond within 30 days. Account deletion can also be initiated directly within the app from the Settings screen.

You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country — for example, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos in Spain (www.aepd.es), the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom (ico.org.uk), or the Information Regulator in South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

7. Cookies and Tracking

Covekin is a mobile application and does not use browser cookies. We may use device identifiers and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Keeping you signed in to your account across sessions
  • Remembering your in-app preferences
  • Collecting anonymised crash and performance data to improve stability (Sentry) and in-app usage analytics (PostHog, EU hosting)

For adult users, we share your device's advertising identifier with our advertising partners only if you allow it (see section 8). We never do this for junior users.

8. Advertising

Covekin may show advertisements. Ads appear only in content feeds, are clearly labelled "Sponsored", and are never shown on voice call screens, in private chats, or on sign-in and sign-up screens.

Junior users (under 18)

Junior users only ever see non-personalised, age-appropriate ads. We tell our advertising partners that the user is under the age of consent, we never share a junior user's advertising identifier, and we never ask junior users for permission to track them.

Adult users (18 and over)

  • By default, adult users see non-personalised ads.
  • Ads are personalised only if you give permission. On iPhone, Apple asks whether you allow Covekin to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. In Europe and the UK, you will also be asked for consent before any personalised ads are shown.
  • If you allow it, our advertising partners may receive your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (country or region), and ad interaction data, and use them to choose which ads to show you.
  • You can change your choice at any time in your device settings (on iPhone, Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking) and in the app's privacy settings.

Our advertising partners will be listed here before ads are shown in the app.
